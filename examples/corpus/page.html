Proof Modes

Three lossless modes

  • dedupe: repeated blocks stored once, repeats become index refs
  • strip: trailing whitespace collapsed, every byte recorded
  • extract: keep matching lines, dropped bytes kept in a sidecar

Guarantee

verify restores the original bytes and compares SHA-256: any mismatch exits 1 as silent-loss.

The workflow name that has no concurrency group in the demo payload is called nightly-audit.
